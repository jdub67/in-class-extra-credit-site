Our Program

Presbyterian College Blue Hose Baseball is the NCAA Division I baseball program representing Presbyterian College in Clinton, South Carolina. The program competes in the Big South Conference and plays its home games at Elton Pollock Field.

Mission

Presbyterian College Blue Hose Baseball exists to compete at the highest level of NCAA Division I baseball while developing student-athletes, creating memorable experiences for fans, and strengthening connections between the team, college, alumni, and community.

Our Story

Blue Hose Baseball represents more than what happens between the lines. Every season brings together student-athletes, coaches, students, families, alumni, and fans who share a connection to Presbyterian College.

Values

Teamwork | Character | Commitment | Community | Pride | Growth
